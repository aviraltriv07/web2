About SciFiLens

Bridging the gap between cinema, theoretical physics, and scientific curiosity

Our Mission

SciFiLens is dedicated to exploring the fascinating intersection between science fiction and real science. We believe that great science fiction elevates our curiosity and drives scientific progress. Our mission is to explain the real physics, astrophysics, biology, and chemistry behind cinema and theoretical questions.

What We Do

We analyze science-fiction movies, theoretical "What If?" scenarios, and core physical principles. Our platform provides:

  • 🌌 WHAT IF? Scenarios: Rigorous 7-stage consequences of theoretical scientific events (e.g. Earth halting its rotation, light-speed travel, solar disappearance).
  • 🎬 Movie Claim vs. Reality: Structured scientific analysis contrasting movie claims with established laws of physics.
  • 🔬 3-Tier Explanations: Multi-depth scientific breakdowns tailored for Beginners, Explorers, and Deep Science enthusiasts.
  • ⚡ Interactive Simulators: Real-time calculators for relativistic time dilation, planetary gravity, and orbital velocity.
  • ⚖️ Scientific Accuracy & Confidence Ratings: Clearly categorizing claims as Established Science, Theoretical, Active Research, Speculative, or Violating Known Physics.

Our Scientific Approach

We consult peer-reviewed physics literature, General Relativity metrics, quantum mechanical bounds, and NASA/ESA mission data. We celebrate cinematic imagination while rigorously respecting the laws of physics and thermodynamics.
